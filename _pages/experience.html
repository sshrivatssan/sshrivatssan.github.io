INTERNSHIPS

Indian Institute of Technology Madras

Summer Research Intern
Integrated Circuits and Systems (iCS) Group, Department of Electrical Engineering
May – July 2026

Conventional digital convolution relies heavily on repeated multiply–accumulate operations and data movement, which can increase power and computational overhead. To address this, I worked on a time-domain analog convolution approach in which controlled continuous-time delays serve as the key building blocks for processing time-series signals. I investigated cascaded first-order all-pass filters to realize an overall delay of approximately 1 µs over a 500 kHz signal bandwidth, and studied both capacitive-feedback and resistive-feedback OTA-based implementations. The resistive-feedback architecture was taken forward for transistor-level realization using a five-transistor OTA in the SKY130A process, with device characterization and gm/ID-based sizing. The final cascaded network was evaluated for its magnitude response and group-delay performance.

Continuous-Time Analog Computing All-Pass Filters OTA Design gm/ID SKY130A

Indian Institute of Science

Research Intern
Param Hansa Centre for Computational Oncology (PHCCO), Bengaluru
June – July 2025

Worked on understanding heterochromatin spreading and epigenetic silencing in Schizosaccharomyces pombe, with emphasis on how three-dimensional chromatin organisation can influence the spatial confinement of H3K9me2 domains. I studied the effect of the Leo1–Paf1 complex and analysed H3K9me2 ChIP-seq data for wild-type and leo1Δ cells together with Hi-C contact maps to examine chromatin organisation and potential structural boundaries to heterochromatin propagation. I also worked with a reaction–diffusion PDE framework to study the spatial behaviour of heterochromatin spreading. This work strengthened my understanding of computational genomics, epigenetic regulation, chromatin-conformation data, and the use of mathematical models to investigate biological processes.

Computational Genomics Hi-C ChIP-seq Reaction–Diffusion Modelling
