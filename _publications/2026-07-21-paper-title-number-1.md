This work presents an ensemble framework for cardiovascular disease (CVD) identification from colour retinal fundus images by integrating handcrafted texture descriptors with deep neural network models. Local Binary Patterns (LBP) and Gray-Level Co-occurrence Matrix (GLCM) features capture complementary retinal texture information and are combined with DenseNet-121, EfficientNet-B3, and MobileNetV3-Large for classification. Evaluated on the China-Fundus-CIMT dataset, the proposed approach achieved improved classification performance over image-only deep learning models while remaining computationally efficient and requiring no additional clinical risk factors or multimodal data.
